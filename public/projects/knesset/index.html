26th Knesset Prediction

From polling measurements to a joint probabilistic forecast of all 120 parliamentary seats.

I built a Bayesian election model that estimates changing voter support and persistent differences between polling series, propagates uncertainty to election day and applies the electoral rules to each simulated outcome.

From observations to parliaments

  1. Curate the measurements. Audit campaign reports and retain eligible national percentage observations. Seat-derived percentages and unsupported subgroup reconstructions are excluded. One partial report contributes only its published ratio.
  2. Estimate changing support. Fit a compositional state-space model with daily latent voting intention, partially pooled pollster effects and measurement covariance in log-ratio coordinates.
  3. Propagate uncertainty. Integrate variance parameters over a 448-point grid using sparse Gaussian inference. Project to election day and add a shared election-level discrepancy.
  4. Allocate seats exactly. Complete the conditional ballot scenario, then apply the 3.25% threshold, six surplus agreements and rational Bader–Ofer allocation to every draw.

The implementation adapts polling components from multiparty election-forecasting research. It uses independent conditional Gaussian draws rather than MCMC and does not fit a fundamentals model or perform MRP.

Assumptions & limits

  • Limited historical calibration. Only one historical national-percentage case, from 2022, updates election-wide discrepancy. The forecast has not demonstrated greater election accuracy than a polling average.
  • Source verification. Most observations are archived secondary transcriptions linked to original reports. Not every primary filing was independently cross-checked.
  • Conditional ballot completion. The reference scenario removes Blue and White proportionally and assigns unmeasured minor lists 0.7% combined support, equally split.
  • Dependence and interpretation. Overlapping polling panels are not separately modeled. Majority probabilities describe seat arithmetic, not government formation.
  • Exact-parliament search. The dashboard’s estimated joint mode is the most probable allocation found in a numerical search, not a certified global optimum.

Reproducible by design

The public repository contains a Python model, curated frozen inputs, locked dependencies, tests, technical documentation and a static dashboard. Its reproduction checker compares all 4,000 issued seat vectors and the recovered terminal mixture with the supplied snapshot.

A separate integration script verifies the probabilities of candidate exact parliaments; it does not repeat the original broad search. The repository records the retrospective holdout results, while the original holdout runner is not redistributed.
